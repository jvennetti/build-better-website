`references/copy-tells.md` is condensed from section 11b of `references/tells.md`
in the `unslop-ui` skill from
[jcarterjohnson/vibecoded-design-tells](https://github.com/jcarterjohnson/vibecoded-design-tells)
(MIT). Tell rankings come from that project's Reddit analysis (~3.2M posts,
2020-2026); the harvested text itself is not redistributed here.

`scripts/copy_scan.py` is a port of the four copy rules (`copy-em-dash`,
`copy-antithesis`, `hype-copy`, `copy-servile`) from that same repo's
`devibe_scan.py` (MIT), narrowed to prose files with a standalone CLI.
